Pandal Through the Years

A journey through our Durga Puja celebrations, pandals and Maa Durga idols over the years.

2025

Durga Puja

A memorable year of devotion and celebration. We tried to replicate Mumbai's Taj hotel to show respect towards Late Ratan Tata passed away in 2024. Also we showcased modern technology use by placing LED screen as background in Mata's Darbar.

2024

Durga Puja

In 2024 we followed a Vrindavan Prem Mandir themed pandal which brought the message of spreading love and avoiding hatred in society. Also in 2024 our pandal changed its colour after every 1 minute with the use of different coloured lighting, making it a unique pandal. We also used every possible resource to make devotees feel like they were in Vrindavan.

2023

Durga Puja

In 2023 we built Chandrayaan 3 space mission model and spacecraft replica themed pandal to celebrate India's successful lunar mission. We also spread public awareness to devotees about the scientists who were involved in this mission through the posters about their work inside pandal hall.

2022

Durga Puja

In 2022 we built UJJAIN Mahakaleshwar Temple replica themed pandal . We intricated temple's architecture design, grand lighting and festive decoration without making a change that attracted devotees from all over chhattisgarh.

2021

Durga Puja

In 2021 we celebrated it during post lockdown/COVID-19 precautionary guidelines. MAA DURGA IDOL and a decent pandal intricated with white thermocol work,creating a clean palace like facade designed to mimic traditional temple structure.
